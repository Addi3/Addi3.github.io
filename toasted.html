Small Q&A!

What does this add / do?

Adds the toaster

Why ?

A. Why not! B. So the toaster in stargate mod wouldnt have more features than needed :D.

Will this mod be ported to forge or neoforge?

No.

How do i make / use the toaster?

It is made like this:

You use it by right-clicking on the block with bread, this will give you toast. If you right-click again (but with toast this time) it will give you burnt toast.

- You can also place it in water :)
