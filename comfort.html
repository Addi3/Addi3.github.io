Comfort is a series of mods that aim to add various Furniture / Decor / Food Items and Blocks to Minecraft.

Why multiple mods?

Well, if this was all under one mod, it would probably be one of the most content-heavy mods in Minecraft. It would also be a massive pain to manage all by myself. Multiple mods also allow people to pick and choose what they want rather than being forced to have hundreds of blocks/items they don’t want.
